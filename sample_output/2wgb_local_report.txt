 3. ESM-SCAN MUTATION SELECTION

 Score = logP(mutant) - logP(wild-type) from ESM-1v; higher is better.
 ESM chooses WHICH amino acid to place at each cryptic position;
 the positions themselves come from the cryptic prediction above.

 Mutation set : charged_polar

 Substitutions are restricted to charged/polar residues on purpose.
 ESM-1v scores evolutionary fitness, so left unrestricted it favours
 whatever preserves the fold -- for a buried hydrophobic residue that
 means another hydrophobic one (ILE -> VAL), which would not open the
 pocket. Burying a charge or polar group is destabilising, which is
 what drives opening, so these scores are expected to be negative.
